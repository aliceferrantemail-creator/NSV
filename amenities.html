Scar Reef

The wave out front is one of Sumbawa’s most respected — a long left that breaks over reef in the bay at Jelenga, and holds its shape when the swell is up.

It is not a secret, but it is not Bali either. Most mornings there is room.

Between sessions

The pool is open all year

It sits between the villas and the garden, in the sun most of the day and in the shade of the hill by late afternoon. There is a terrace with chairs, and enough lawn to leave a board on.

Practical things

Getting here
Sultan Muhammad Kaharuddin III Airport (SMKIII) in Sumbawa Besar is about 132 km away. We can arrange a transfer — ask when you book, it is charged separately.
Connection
Starlink across the property. Free private parking on site.
Laundry & kitchen
Washing machine, fridge, and a kitchen you are welcome to use.
Check in & out
Arrive between 15:00 and 18:00. Leave between 08:00 and 11:00. Tell us if your flight makes that awkward.
House rules
Children are welcome. No pets. Non-smoking. No parties or stag/hen groups.
Nearby
Jelenga village is small and local, with warungs a short walk away and a beach that does good sunsets.
