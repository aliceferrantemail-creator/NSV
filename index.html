A quiet bay on the west coast of Sumbawa

Nira Surf Villas is a two-villa retreat at Beru, Jereweh — thatch and stone set back from the sand, with the hills rising behind and a pool between you and the garden.

Out front is Scar Reef, one of Sumbawa’s most respected waves. Behind you, not much at all. That is rather the point.

In both villas
  • Air conditioning
  • Hot showers
  • Starlink wi-fi
  • 11-metre pool
  • Fridge
  • Laundry service
